Common questions

Is InternHub free for students?

Yes. Create an account, complete your profile, and start applying.

What is an internal vs external apply?

Internal apply is tracked inside InternHub with an application ID. External apply opens the original company URL.

How do I hear about status changes?

Check My Applications after login. Status updates are also sent to your email.
